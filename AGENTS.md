# SmartRoute — Agent Context

Thesis project (deadline: **early December 2026**). Multi-city trip optimizer: backend finds the cheapest city visiting order with real flight prices; mobile app plans per-city itineraries. Solo developer (RyoFallen); trunk-based development on `main`.

- **Phase 7 in progress (2026-08-09).** Design decisions are settled and recorded, and split into nine tickets (#24–#32, parent spec #23). **#24 is done**: `Attraction.openingHours` (OSM `opening_hours` syntax) and `Attraction.visitMinutes` exist, populated by a three-file seed join — the hand-curated `attractions.json` plus an ODbL-marked `opening-hours.osm.json` and an `opening-hours.manual.json`, joined on city code + exact attraction name, with orphans, duplicate provenance and invalid grammar all failing the seed. `visitMinutes` falls back per `AttractionCategory` at **read** time (`visit-duration.ts`) so curated and assumed values stay countable. Note `PH`/`SH` holiday macros are rejected on purpose: the parser cannot evaluate them for `tr` or `gb` (Istanbul, London), so curated data uses explicit dates. **#26 is done** (the tracer bullet): `GET /trips/:id/itinerary` returns the day-grouped Itinerary, computed on demand by a pure `generateItinerary` (`services/itinerary/generate.ts`) — no DB, no network, no clock — over a `WalkingDistanceProvider` boundary (`WALKING_PROVIDER=mock|osrm`; only the deterministic haversine-plus-detour mock exists until #28). SchedulableWindows come from real `FlightLeg` datetimes (arrival + 90 min transfer, departure − 180 min airport, day end 21:00, floored at the profile wake-up time); open-tour asymmetry and empty days with a typed `EmptyDayReason` are handled. Times are wall-clock in each city's own zone, emitted as ISO with that offset (`services/itinerary/zoned.ts`, `Intl`-based, DST-correct). Mobile's flat plan view became `ItineraryScreen`/`ItineraryViewModel`/`ItineraryRoute` (the glossary reserves "plan" for the flat must-go list) with day sections, walking gaps and localized empty-day reasons; `GET /trips/:id/plan` stays untouched but mobile no longer calls it. **#27 is done**: `optimalWalkingRoute` (`services/itinerary/tour.ts`) solves an exact minimum-cost Hamiltonian **path** — not a cycle, the traveler does not walk home — by Held–Karp over the walking matrix, read as directed so an asymmetric OSRM matrix needs no special case; ties break toward the lexicographically smallest route, which means editorial rank breaks distance ties and the result is stable. Note the glossary split recorded in CONTEXT.md: _visiting order_ stays the **city** order (the GA's), _WalkingRoute_ is the intra-city one. The city's whole route is one queue consumed from the front, so ItineraryDays are contiguous stretches of it by construction, and a `DEFAULT_MAX_SLOTS_PER_DAY = 6` ceiling (threaded through `ItineraryInput.maxSlotsPerDay` for #29 to drive from TripIntensity) stops a twelve-hour day swallowing the city. Optimality is pinned against a permutation oracle at n=2..8, symmetric and asymmetric, the oracle scoring routes with its own cost function so a wrong answer cannot cancel against a wrong scorer; on the real Istanbul seed the route walks 171 minutes against editorial rank's 346, and that pair is pinned. `MAX_EXACT_TOUR_NODES = 15` is where Held–Karp stops being instant (~7M ops over a 3.9 MB table; n=20 would be ~400M over 168 MB); `optimalWalkingRoute` throws above it rather than degrading quietly, and a seed-data test holds the curated cities under it. **Known deviation:** filling days from the front of the route is not balancing them — twelve attractions over three days give 6/6/0, not 4/4/4 — because the ceiling is the only thing stopping day one. Choosing between the valid cuts is #30's job (it scores them against weekly closures, with walking distance only as its tie-break), so the wart closes there rather than being patched here. **#29 is done**: `selectAttractions` (`services/itinerary/select.ts`) decides _which_ attractions a stay holds before anything is routed — `usableDays × ceiling` of them, or fewer if the city has fewer — ranking liked InterestCategories up, disliked down (so they are dropped first) and letting editorial rank break every tie. `ATTRACTION_TO_INTEREST_CATEGORY` gets its first consumer, which is what makes the personalisation claim true rather than aspirational; a linear scan is deliberate at ≤15 interests × ≤15 attractions. TripIntensity now drives the ceiling through `MAX_SLOTS_PER_DAY_BY_INTENSITY` (RELAXED 4 / MODERATE 6 / PACKED 8; `ItineraryInput.maxSlotsPerDay` is gone, replaced by `intensity` + `interests`, both optional and defaulting to MODERATE/none for a traveler who has not onboarded — `ItineraryService` passes the profile's values straight through rather than defaulting twice). **`usableDays`, not calendar days, is load-bearing**: a day the flights closed before it opened (dawn departure, midnight landing) must not raise the limit, or the limit stops binding, and the surplus then gets trimmed by the walking route running out of day rather than by what the traveler likes — which silently un-personalises exactly the trips that have an early flight in them. A day left with a genuine but tiny sliver still counts, because whether a specific visit fits it is not knowable before the route is. A regression test pins this (`does not let a day the flights wiped out buy attractions nobody will see`) and it is deliberately built on a road-shaped walking matrix: with a uniform matrix the route preserves selection order and the bug is invisible. Selection order — not editorial rank — is what feeds the walking solver, so the solver only ever sees the attractions the traveler will be shown (an incidental win: a city with more attractions than `MAX_EXACT_TOUR_NODES` is reachable where the ceiling cuts it back below that bound) and equal-cost walks break toward what the traveler wants more. Note what Interests do _not_ do: the order within a day is still the shortest walking route's, by #27's design — Interests decide who is on the list, not who is seen first. Still naive by design: OpeningHours are carried for disclosure but never consulted when placing a day (#30/#31). **#28 is done**: `OsrmWalkingDistanceProvider` (`services/itinerary/osrm.ts`) is the second implementation of the `WalkingDistanceProvider` boundary, selected by `WALKING_PROVIDER=osrm` with `OSRM_BASE_URL`; the mock stays the default so `pnpm test` and CI never need a container. It hits `/table/v1/foot` (**lon,lat** order — reversed from every `Coordinate` in this codebase), converts seconds to whole minutes with the same rounding as the mock so switching providers changes the numbers and nothing else, keeps the matrix asymmetric because #27 reads it as directed, and degrades one `null` pair to the straight-line estimate (summarised in a single log line per table, not per cell) rather than failing an Itinerary over one unreachable attraction. The extract recipe is `apps/backend/scripts/osrm/` — pinned Geofabrik **dated** snapshot `260815` (never `-latest`, or a measured walking time stops being re-derivable), bounding boxes **derived from `attractions.json`** rather than hand-listed so they cannot drift behind the dataset, clipped and merged, then MLD-preprocessed; the extract is gitignored and `PROVENANCE.txt` records snapshot, image tag, boxes and source checksums. Two things were learned by actually running it, both recorded in `scripts/osrm/README.md`: the clip uses **osmconvert, not `osmium extract`**, because osmium is OOM-killed on a country-sized region in the 2 GB Docker VM that both colima and Docker Desktop default to on macOS; and the profile is **`foot-smartroute.lua`, the stock foot profile with ferries removed** — stock OSRM routed Galata Tower → Dolmabahçe Palace over a Bosphorus sightseeing cruise at 48 km/h and called it walking, which is not a WalkingRoute under CONTEXT.md's own definition. Verified live (`osrm.live.test.ts`, skipped unless `WALKING_PROVIDER=osrm`): Hagia Sophia → Galata Tower walks 541 m across **Galata Köprüsü**, 3.17 km on foot against 2.09 km straight (1.52×, 39 min vs the mock's 33), all 66 seeded Istanbul pairs travel at 5.0 km/h, and a same-bank control stays inside the mock's detour factor. Note the ticket's "roughly double" estimate did not survive measurement: for the pair it named (Süleymaniye → Galata Tower) the real walk is 1.89 km against 1.34 km, only 1.41×, because the Galata Bridge sits almost on the direct line — and the largest detour in the whole seeded set is not water at all but Hagia Sophia → Topkapı Palace (0.35 km apart, 1.28 km on foot, 3.62×, walled palace grounds). Everything below is the agreed design for the remaining tickets. ADR-0005 excludes Google Maps Platform entirely (storage clauses forbid persisting attraction data; §14.2/§19.2 forbid pairing its content with MapLibre; the Directions API is Legacy and closed to new Cloud projects) — the geo stack is OSM end to end: Overpass for opening hours, self-hosted OSRM (foot) for pedestrian distances, in-process exact TSP for ordering, MapLibre unchanged. Agreed design, superseding spec §7.3.1's k-means: **route-first, cluster-second** (one exact Held–Karp TSP per city, tour sliced into contiguous per-day segments) with **exhaustive scoring over all valid cut vectors** (≤161 candidates; minimise weekly-closure conflicts, tie-break on walking distance). `TripIntensity` is a per-day **ceiling, not a quota** — where a city has fewer attractions than the ceiling (all 8 cities hold ~12, so this binds past ~2 days), `Interest` ratings select and rank, which finally gives `ATTRACTION_TO_INTEREST_CATEGORY` its first consumer and makes the personalisation claim true. Itineraries are **computed on demand** (pure function of persisted inputs — deliberately no `CityItinerary` table, contra spec §5.2). Day windows come from **actual `FlightLeg` datetimes plus transfer buffers**, not spec §7.3.1's fixed half-days; note trips are **open tours** (`legs.length === order.length - 1`), so the origin city has no arrival and the last city no departure. Unknown `OpeningHours` get a documented per-category assumed window, disclosed to the client. `VisitDuration` is our own curated datum with a per-`AttractionCategory` fallback — no provider publishes it. Round scope: backend + `TripPlanScreen` restructured to day-grouped slots; **real map rendering stays deferred** (still ADR-0004's own slice). - **#30 implemented (2026-08-17), ahead of #25 rather than after it.** `generateItinerary` no longer fills days from the front of the walking route; `arrange.ts` picks how the route is divided across the stay's days, so an Attraction shut on a Monday lands on a day it is open wherever any arrangement allows. **Dynamic programming over (day, position in the route), not enumeration** — that was the first implementation and it was wrong: divisions grow combinatorially in days (15 attractions over 5 days is 1,451, over 7 days 33,390, a fortnight is millions), so it needed a cap, and above the cap it fell back to front-filling — a 7-day Rome stay silently became `6 6 3 0 0 0 0` with four days reading "nothing left to visit". The DP is exact at every stay length and runs in ~60ms at 14 days. Scoring is lexicographic and the order is load-bearing: **coverage → closures → walking → evenness**. Coverage leads because a day's share is a _cap, not a quota_ (a day whose window cannot hold its share schedules less), so ranking closures first would let the generator quietly drop an attraction to dodge a locked door — trading a disclosed problem for a hidden one. Evenness exists because **walking alone is biased, not merely arbitrary**: only the second visit of a day onwards carries a walk (the first is reached from wherever the traveler slept, which this model does not know), so every extra day used silently removes a charge and "least walking" always favours spreading thin — twelve attractions over three days came out **1/5/6** before evenness was added, and is **4/4/4** now, closing the 6/6/0 wart #27 recorded. Knock-on: the pinned seed walking figures dropped from 171/346 to **63/224**, not because routing improved but because a stay now uses all its days, which charges fewer walks for the rank baseline exactly as much as for the route. `closures.ts` answers "is it shut all day" via the `opening_hours` library, with `24/7` short-circuited and **null never a closure** (unknown is a fact about our data, not the attraction). It builds its `Date` with the **local** constructor, never `Date.UTC` — the library reads local getters, and a UTC-built instant evaluates the wrong weekday on a machine far enough east or west; pinned under UTC+14 and UTC−11. Conflicts no arrangement can avoid are flagged `closedOnThisDay` and badged in `ItineraryScreen`. New `EmptyDayReason.SCHEDULED_AROUND_CLOSURES` covers a usable day left clear on purpose, ordered _after_ the flight reasons since a flight that wrecked a day is why the arrangement had nothing to put on it. **Known deviation from the ticket:** the AC says "where any valid cut avoids all closure conflicts, the returned Itinerary has none", but coverage outranks conflicts — where the only conflict-free arrangement shows the traveler less of the city, the fuller one wins and the conflict is disclosed instead. **Reality check: only two attractions in the whole 95-attraction dataset can currently close a day** — Topkapı (Tuesdays) and the Grand Bazaar (Sundays), both from OSM, and both pinned by a seed-data test. The machinery is correct but stays largely inert until #25 fills the other 82.

## Non-obvious config (learned the hard way — don't "fix" these)

- `TokenStore` on iOS is backed by the **Keychain** (`KeychainSettings(service = "com.smartroute.traveler")`), which **survives deleting the app**. Uninstalling and reinstalling on the simulator does _not_ clear a session — `xcrun simctl keychain <device> reset` does. Android is `SharedPreferences` (`smartroute_auth`) and clears with the app, so an auth bug can look iOS-only when it is not. Staging an auth state on Android is easy (`adb push` a prefs XML, then `run-as <pkg> cp` it into `shared_prefs/`); on iOS it is not — prefer Android for that kind of test.
- `mobile/build.gradle.kts`: ktlint engine is pinned to **1.5.0** — required for the `.editorconfig` `ktlint_function_naming_ignore_when_annotated_with = Composable` exemption to work. Generated Compose resources are excluded via a **predicate** filter (`contains("/build/generated/")`); glob excludes silently fail for registered generated source roots.
- `iosMain/MainViewController.kt`: the entry point must stay PascalCase `MainViewController()` (Swift calls `MainViewControllerKt.MainViewController()`); it carries a ktlint `@Suppress` on purpose.
- Map: MapLibre (`demotiles` style, no token) was chosen for the spike; Mapbox swap is an open styling decision (spec §8.3). `RouteMap` is `expect`/`actual`; evolve it toward a state-holder parameter when adding polylines/markers, don't accrete positional params.
- CI: `gradle/actions/setup-gradle@v4` validates the committed Gradle wrapper JAR. `mobile/gradlew` must keep its executable bit (`100755`).
- Duffel test mode returns only the synthetic "Duffel Airways" carrier; currency mapper prefers USD, falls back to cheapest — a real FX layer is a known follow-up.
- OSRM is published on **host port 5050**, not its own 5000 (`docker-compose.yml`, `OSRM_BASE_URL`). macOS's AirPlay Receiver listens on 5000 on both IPv4 and IPv6 and answers **403 with an empty body** to everything, which is indistinguishable from a broken OSRM container until you look at the `Server: AirTunes` header. Keep compose, `env.ts`, `plugins/walking.ts` and `.env.example` in step if this ever moves.
- The OSRM service is behind a compose **profile** (`docker compose --profile osrm up -d osrm`). It mounts a gitignored graph directory, so without the profile a fresh clone's `docker compose up -d` would crash-loop on a missing extract. Plain `docker compose up -d` still means "postgres" and nothing else.
- Navigation-Compose type-safe routes (pinned at `2.8.0-alpha12`): a route data class field can only be a built-in primitive (`String`/`Int`/`Boolean`/`Float`/`Long`) or a `List` of those — **never a custom enum or other `@Serializable` type**, even though that works fine for network DTOs. There's no `NavType` for anything else, and the failure isn't a compile error — it's a runtime crash the instant the `NavHost` composes (eagerly builds every registered route), not when you navigate to the offending one. If a route needs an enum, store `theEnum.name: String` on the route and convert back to the enum where the route is consumed (see `ResultsRoute.preset` / commit `a2ffdad`). No unit test in this repo would catch this — nothing here constructs a real `NavHost`.
- iOS URL opening (`MapOpener.ios.kt`): use `UIApplication.openURL(url, options, completionHandler)`, **never the one-argument `openURL(_:)`**. On current iOS (observed on 26.5) UIKit force-returns `false` for the deprecated form and logs `BUG IN CLIENT OF UIKIT`, so the tap silently does nothing — no crash, no compile error, and no test here would notice. Found on the iOS 26.5 simulator during #32; Android's `geo:` intent was never affected.
